The Project

Building the operational infrastructure behind effective marketing — workflows, automation, systems and the documentation that makes them stick.

Good marketing output depends on good marketing operations. Across client work and my own studio, I've designed and implemented the systems that sit behind campaigns — AI-assisted workflows that reduce repetitive work, CRM automation that keeps outreach consistent, Notion-based knowledge bases that stop information living in someone's inbox, and process documentation that means new team members can get up to speed without needing to ask. The goal is always the same: less friction, more output, nothing falling through the cracks.
