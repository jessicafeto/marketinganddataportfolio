The Project

Translating complex AI and financial services topics into content that builds credibility and drives engagement.

I wrote and produced content across every channel Intellimation used — LinkedIn, the company website, product brochures, presentations and executive communications. The work generated 33,900 LinkedIn impressions and over 300 reactions across nine months. The challenge throughout was the same: take highly technical AI concepts and make them compelling, clear and relevant to financial services audiences who care more about business outcomes than technology architecture — the same translation work that any complex, technical or regulated category needs from its content.
