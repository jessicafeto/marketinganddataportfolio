Welcome to OPA Taverne

A table set the way it's always been — just with better light.

OPA began as a family taverna: a blue-washed wall, a handful of tables, and recipes carried over from a grandmother's kitchen in Attica. We haven't changed the food. We've changed how it's served — slower, quieter, with the same care you'd bring to a table in your own courtyard.

Every plate still starts with the same three things: good oil, good bread, and good company. What's different is the room around it — stone, linen, warm evening light, and enough space to actually taste what you're eating.

The Dimitriou Family Founders, OPA Taverne
