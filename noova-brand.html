The Project

Building Noova from first principles: a complete brand, a digital experience, and the systems to make both work.

Noova is a data and marketing studio I founded. Every element of it, the name, the positioning, the identity, the website, the marketing strategy, came from a blank page. I defined what the brand stood for before touching a single design file: who it was for, how it should feel, what it would never be. From there I built the visual identity, designed and developed the site, structured the SEO foundations, integrated AI tools into the workflow, and created the marketing strategy that would carry it forward. This is the project I know inside out, because I am the client and the studio.

Brand Strategy & Identity System

Positioning, naming, voice and visual identity, built from a strategic brief, not a mood board.

The brand started with a strategic question: what kind of studio earns the trust of businesses that take data seriously? The name Noova, an invented word, clean and memorable, came from that brief. The identity followed: a restrained palette, editorial typography pairing Söhne with Tiempos, and a tone of voice that treats clients as intelligent.

Digital Experience & Marketing Systems

A website designed and built to express the brand: from architecture and UX to AI-integrated workflows and SEO.

The site was designed and built in code: Tailwind, GSAP animations, deployed on Vercel. Every page decision traced back to brand strategy: how it should feel to arrive, how work should be presented, where the CTA should live and why. SEO foundations, structured content and AI-assisted workflows were built in from the start, not retrofitted.
