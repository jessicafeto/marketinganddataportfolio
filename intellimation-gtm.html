The challenge

A sophisticated AI product serving four different BFSI verticals — with nothing to hand a prospect after a meeting.

No Sales Collateral No Positioning Framework "AI" Too Generic Four Verticals One Product Sceptical Senior Buyers
What I delivered

Framework first, then four pieces of collateral that each speak a buyer's language.

01 · Positioning Framework

One shared framework — Vertical AI vs. GenAI — across all four brochures

Defined the single positioning spine: Symphony is a Vertical AI built for finance, not a generic GenAI tool. This distinction anchored every brochure and every sales conversation.

  • Vertical AI vs. GenAI positioning defined
  • Three buyer personas mapped (Ops, Tech, C-suite)
  • BFSI-specific proof points identified
02 · Vertical AI Overview

Flagship brochure introducing the product across all audiences simultaneously

The first document any prospect received — clear positioning statement, Symphony's core value proposition, and three workflow examples showing what it actually does.

  • Positioning statement & value prop
  • Three workflow examples in plain English
  • Written for non-technical C-suite readers
03 · Vertical Brochures (×3)

Three persona-led brochures — Structured Products, Direct Lending, Prime Brokerage

Each vertical brochure led with that team's specific pain points before introducing Symphony's solution — ops teams, portfolio monitors, and collateral desks all got a different framing.

  • Pain-first → solution structure
  • Vertical-specific use cases only
  • Jargon matched to the buyer's own language
04 · Design & Publishing

Laid out in Canva and deployed across events and sales meetings

Designed to the existing brand toolkit, published as PDFs for email and print-ready files for conference distribution at SRP Europe and the Collateral Management Summit.

  • Canva layout to brand toolkit
  • Print & digital versions published
  • Deployed at SRP Europe & Collateral Mgmt Summit
