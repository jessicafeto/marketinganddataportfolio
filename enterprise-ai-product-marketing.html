The Project

Built Intellimation's product marketing function from zero — as the only person in the role, reporting directly to the CEO and CFO.

Intellimation.ai builds proprietary AI for banking and financial services — automation for an industry that runs on trust, sold to buyers who don't take technical claims at face value. When I joined, the product was strong but there was no product marketing function behind it: no consistent positioning, no enterprise-ready asset library, just a commercial team running roughly five demos a week without a system to support them. I built one — a repeatable messaging framework across four Vertical AI product lines (Structured Products, Collateral Management, Direct Lending and Data-Aware AI), designed and produced in Figma, reviewed with the CEO and Director of AI Products for technical accuracy, and fed by a Voice of the Customer programme I started from scratch. The result was material that could stand next to institutions like Barclays, Citi and Nomura — credible, clear, and built to keep improving with every conversation it supported.
