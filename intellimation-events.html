The Project

Planning, coordinating and marketing executive events for an enterprise AI company in financial services.

Intellimation.ai participated in QuantMinds International and QuantForum — two of the most senior gatherings in quantitative finance. I managed the full event lifecycle: exhibition stand design and production, event branding, printed collateral, invitations, attendee communications, on-the-day logistics and post-event follow-up. The result was a consistent, professional presence at events attended by heads of quant, CTOs and senior executives from global banks.
