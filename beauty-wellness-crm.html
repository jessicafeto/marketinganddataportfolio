Designed a Customer Lifecycle and Retention Programme for a Beauty & Wellness Brand

Beauty and wellness brands have some of the most emotionally engaged customers of any category and one of the highest risks of churn if the post-purchase experience doesn't match the brand promise. This concept designs a full customer lifecycle strategy: how to welcome a new customer, build a habit, recognise loyal customers, and win back lapsed ones using segmentation, CRM flows, and channel-appropriate messaging.

Type Concept project strategy
Sector Beauty / Wellness / Aesthetics
Disciplines CRM · Lifecycle Marketing · Segmentation
Output Lifecycle strategy · CRM flows · Segmentation model
01 The brief

Beauty customers are loyal to the feeling, not the product. CRM is how you protect it.

The average beauty brand loses 60–70% of first-time buyers before a second purchase. The moment between a first purchase and the second visit is the highest-risk point in the customer lifecycle and the point where most brands go quiet, relying on the product alone to do the retention work.

A well-designed lifecycle strategy intervenes at each stage: onboarding the new customer, celebrating loyalty, re-engaging the lapsed one, and converting occasional buyers into brand advocates. This project maps those five stages and designs the CRM approach for each.

02 Lifecycle stages

Five stages. Five different jobs for CRM to do.

01

Welcome & Onboard

New customer, first purchase. CRM job: make the first experience memorable. Welcome sequence (email + SMS), product education, expectation-setting. Goal: get them excited for the result before they've seen it.

02

Second Purchase Push

Customer has completed the first experience. CRM job: strike before inertia sets in. Timely follow-up (10–14 days post-purchase), social proof from customers with similar profiles, targeted next-product recommendation. Goal: convert first-timers into repeat buyers.

03

Habit Building

Customer has bought twice. CRM job: turn repeat purchase into routine. Subscription or replenishment nudge, loyalty programme introduction, exclusive early-access offers. Goal: make purchasing from this brand the path of least resistance.

04

Loyalty & Advocacy

High-frequency, high-spend customers. CRM job: make them feel seen. Personalised recognition (anniversary of first purchase, top-spender perks), referral programme, community content. Goal: convert loyal customers into active brand advocates who bring others in.

05

Win-Back

Previously active customer who has gone quiet. CRM job: give them a reason to return before they've consciously decided to leave. Personalised re-engagement, product update or reformulation announcement, time-limited offer. Goal: reactivate before churn becomes permanent.

03 Segmentation model

Customer segments that make targeting decisions automatic.

Each lifecycle stage is defined by behavioural signals purchase date, frequency, average order value, product category, and engagement with emails/SMS. The segmentation model assigns customers to stages dynamically, so CRM flows trigger based on actual behaviour rather than fixed time windows.

Behavioural signals used

Days since last purchase, number of purchases in last 90 days, average order value, product category affinity, email open rate, SMS click rate, and referral history. These signals are scored to produce a composite loyalty score and a churn risk score for each customer.

CRM flow triggers

Segments trigger automatically when behaviour crosses defined thresholds e.g., 28 days since last purchase triggers the "at-risk" re-engagement flow; 3+ purchases in 90 days triggers the loyalty recognition flow. No manual campaign scheduling required once configured.

Why lifecycle CRM is a priority for beauty & wellness brands

In beauty and wellness, repeat purchase and word-of-mouth are the primary growth engines not paid acquisition. A well-designed CRM lifecycle strategy directly increases LTV, reduces churn, and turns one-time buyers into advocates who bring new customers in at no acquisition cost. This project demonstrates the ability to design that strategy end-to-end: from segmentation logic to channel sequencing to messaging principles at each stage.
