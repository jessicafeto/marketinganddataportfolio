About

Hi, I'm Jessica.

I hold an MSc in Data Science & Analytics (University of Westminster — dissertation on data-driven fashion analytics) and an MSc in Computer Engineering, alongside hands-on experience across digital marketing, CRM, content strategy and reporting. That combination means I'm equally comfortable writing a campaign brief, building a customer segmentation model, producing sales collateral for a technical product, or putting together the dashboard that proves it all worked.

My work spans two very different worlds: consumer brands (fashion, beauty, hospitality) and B2B fintech — most recently as the marketing consultant behind the content, collateral and event strategy for a Vertical AI platform selling into banks and asset managers.

I'm currently based in the UK on a Skilled Worker visa and looking for my next role — ideally somewhere I can bring marketing strategy and data analysis together, in marketing or data, for a consumer brand or a B2B/fintech platform.
