The Project

Turning marketing data into decisions — through dashboards, campaign reporting and customer insight work that connects activity to outcomes.

Across client and agency work I built and maintained the analytics infrastructure that makes marketing legible. GA4 and Looker Studio for web and campaign performance, Power BI and Excel for business reporting, SQL for deeper data pulls. The output was always the same: a clear picture of what the numbers meant and what to do next. KPI dashboards gave stakeholders a live view; campaign reports gave teams a clean record of performance; customer insight work fed directly into content strategy and targeting decisions.

Campaign Reporting

Tracking performance across paid, organic and owned channels — structured so teams know what to keep, cut or scale.
