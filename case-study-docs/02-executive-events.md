## Business Context

Intellimation.ai is a fast-growing Vertical AI company. Because the product serves a highly specialised enterprise BFSI audience, buying decisions are relationship-driven and involve senior stakeholders — so executive events were a **core go-to-market channel**, not a side activity: they educated the market, built trust, demonstrated thought leadership, and created direct engagement with qualified prospects. Rather than relying solely on outbound sales, Intellimation.ai combined executive events, targeted marketing, thought leadership and personalised follow-up to support enterprise pipeline generation and strengthen relationships with existing and prospective clients.

## My Role

Part of a small team working directly with the CEO and commercial leadership — not the sole owner of the programme, but the owner/coordinator of the marketing and operational execution across all four events:

- Executive invitation campaigns and attendee communications
- RSVP management and attendee tracking
- Event planning documentation (master checklist: venue, registration, exhibition booth, branding, speaker logistics)
- Marketing collateral and digital assets (branding, brochures, presentation materials)
- Coordination across stakeholders
- Follow-up communications and CRM updates/reporting
- Supporting the overall attendee experience

**Budget ownership and wider commercial decisions sat with leadership** — Jessica was responsible for delivering and coordinating marketing/operational execution, not for the commercial strategy or spend decisions. ## Obstacles

Lean budget and limited internal resources — Jessica was one of the primary people responsible for marketing, balancing invitations, communications, collateral, logistics, RSVP management and post-event follow-up simultaneously across multiple events, in a small, fast-moving team, without compromising the standard expected by a senior enterprise audience.

## Reflection

**What she's most proud of:** creating a consistent executive experience across multiple events rather than treating each as a standalone campaign — repeatable marketing workflows, communication processes and event assets that made engagement more structured and scalable.

**What she'd do differently:** introduce a standardised event measurement framework from day one — consistent KPIs, CRM attribution and post-event reporting applied the same way across every event, to make performance comparable and commercial impact measurable over time.
