The Project

Built the marketing infrastructure for a Vertical AI platform serving global financial institutions.

Intellimation.ai builds proprietary AI for banking and financial services — automation purpose-built for an industry that runs on trust. I led product positioning, collateral design and sales enablement across four BFSI verticals: Structured Products, Collateral Management, Direct Lending and Data-Aware AI. The goal was material that could stand next to institutions like Barclays, Citi and Nomura — credible, clear and ready for the room it was selling into.
