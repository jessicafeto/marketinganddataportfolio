Segmented Hotel Guests by Behaviour and Value to Reveal Retention and Revenue Opportunities

Using the Kaggle Hotel Booking Demand dataset (119,000+ real hotel bookings), this concept project applies RFM segmentation to identify a hotel property's most valuable guest types who books most frequently, who spends the most, and who is most at risk of not returning. The output is a Power BI dashboard designed to support revenue management and CRM decisions.

Type Concept project public dataset
Sector Hospitality / Hotels
Tools Python · Power BI · RFM Analysis
Output Segmentation model · Power BI dashboard
01 Why this project

Hotels have more customer data than almost any other business. Most don't use it well.

A hotel property has a complete picture of its guests: booking frequency, lead time, length of stay, room type, spend, cancellation behaviour, and channel origin. Most of this data sits in a PMS or booking system and is never used for customer-level segmentation despite being a goldmine for retention and revenue decisions.

This project applies the same RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation framework used in retail and e-commerce CRM to a hospitality dataset producing guest segments that directly inform CRM strategy, loyalty programme design, and targeted outreach.

02 Dataset & approach

119,390 hotel bookings. Two property types. Real cancellation behaviour.

Dataset

Kaggle Hotel Booking Demand dataset 119,390 rows of real hotel booking records across a city hotel and a resort hotel. Includes booking date, lead time, stay duration, room type, ADR (average daily rate), market segment, cancellation status, and more.

RFM segmentation

Recency (days since last booking), Frequency (number of bookings), and Monetary (total spend) scores calculated per guest. Guests grouped into five segments: Champions, Loyalists, Potential Loyalists, At-Risk, and Churned.

Power BI dashboard

Interactive dashboard showing segment distribution, ADR by segment, cancellation rate by segment, and booking channel breakdown. Designed to answer: where is revenue at risk, and which guests should receive CRM outreach first?

03 Guest segments

Five segments. Five different CRM responses.

01

Champions

Highest frequency, most recent, highest spend. These guests book without prompting and rarely cancel. Priority: acknowledge loyalty, offer early access, avoid over-discounting.

02

Loyalists

Frequent, recent, and high-value not quite Champions but close. Priority: move them into Champion territory with targeted offers and loyalty programme visibility.

03

Potential Loyalists

Recent bookings but not yet frequent. Priority: second-stay incentive (post-checkout email with returning guest offer) to build the repeat behaviour.

04

At-Risk

Previously frequent or high-spend guests who haven't booked recently. Priority: win-back campaign personalised outreach referencing their previous stays.

05

Churned

No bookings in a long period, low frequency. Priority: low-cost re-engagement campaign (seasonal offer) or accept churn and reallocate spend to higher-value segments.

04 Tools & skills

What this project demonstrates.

Python (pandas, scikit-learn) RFM analysis Customer segmentation Power BI Retention strategy CRM strategy Revenue management thinking Hospitality analytics Data storytelling

Why hospitality analytics matters

Customer segmentation is one of the highest-ROI activities a hotel, restaurant, or hospitality group can invest in it determines who gets loyalty perks, who gets win-back emails, and where CRM budget should focus. This project demonstrates the ability to take raw booking data and turn it into segment-specific action plans that a marketing or revenue team can act on immediately.
