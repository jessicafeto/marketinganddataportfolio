## 1. What Top Marketing Portfolios Must Demonstrate

Recruiters and hiring managers — particularly at tech companies, B2B SaaS, fintech, and agencies — look for portfolios that answer four questions fast:

1. **Can you drive results?** Numbers, not descriptions.
2. **Do you have range?** Multiple channels and disciplines, not just one trick.
3. **Can you think strategically?** Evidence of owning the brief, not just executing tasks.
4. **Do you understand the audience?** B2B vs B2C, senior vs junior buyer, technical vs commercial.

The specific disciplines recruiters want to see evidenced depend on the role type. For marketing analytics, product marketing, and senior marketing roles in tech/fintech (the most likely visa-sponsoring employers in the UK), the priority list looks like this:

### Tier 1 — Must-Haves
| Discipline | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Content strategy with measurable reach | Proves you can build an audience, not just write |
| Campaign performance & analytics | Data literacy is now a core marketing expectation |
| Go-to-market / product marketing | High demand in B2B tech; strong salary signal |
| CRM & lifecycle marketing | Every scale-up needs retention as much as acquisition |
| Cross-channel reporting & dashboards | Shows you can own the measurement layer |

### Tier 2 — Strong Differentiators
| Discipline | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Paid media (Google/Meta) | Even if limited, evidence of budget ownership matters |
| Email & automation | Table stakes at any growth-stage company |
| Sales enablement & collateral | Needed at most B2B companies; rare in pure marketing portfolios |
| Event marketing | High-value, hard to fake, shows end-to-end ownership |
| AI/technical marketing | Major differentiator in 2025–2026; very few marketers can evidence this |

### Tier 3 — Bonus for Data/Tech Roles
| Discipline | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Python / data science | Shows you can pull your own data, not wait for analysts |
| NLP & sentiment analysis | Directly applicable to audience research and social listening |
| Power BI / Looker dashboards | Proves you can present data to non-technical stakeholders |
| Product enablement & UAT | Bridges marketing and product; rare and valued |
| Competitive & market analysis | Shows strategic thinking at a business level |

## 3. Gap Analysis

### What You Have That's Rare & Strong
- **B2B enterprise marketing at Tier-1 client level** (JPMorgan, BofA, Barclays) — almost no marketer at your level has this.
- **AI/Vertical tech marketing experience** — a genuine differentiator in 2025–2026.
- **End-to-end ownership across multiple channels** — content, events, collateral, CRM, data.
- **Technical data skills** (Python, SQL, Power BI, GA4) — most marketers can't do this.
- **Forbes credibility** — external validation that is instantly recognisable.

### What's Missing or Weak

| Gap | Priority | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid media with real spend and results** | High | Real campaigns are hard to evidence without access. A concept with realistic budget, audience, ROAS targets, and a platform-level explanation carries more weight than you'd expect. |
| **SEO evidence** | Medium | A technical SEO audit or content-led SEO strategy case study would round out the digital marketing coverage. |
| **Email with real metrics** | Medium | Open rate, CTR, conversion. Even a single campaign result would upgrade the Ritual Email concept. |
| **B2C consumer brand work with real results** | Medium | All real work is B2B. Haus Labs and Ritual fill this but as concepts only. |
| **Campaign from brief to measurable outcome** | High | The strongest portfolio projects show: here was the brief → here's the strategy → here's what happened. CS01 and CS05 do this well. More of this format needed. |
| **Stakeholder management / leadership evidence** | Medium | Evidenced in the case studies but not called out clearly enough. Rename and reframe where possible. |
