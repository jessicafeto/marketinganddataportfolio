The challenge

A packed events calendar — but no single owner turning it into a repeatable marketing motion.

No Events Owner No CRM Follow-Through Two Formats, No Framework Multi-City Logistics
What I delivered

End-to-end ownership across invites, logistics, execution, and pipeline follow-through.

01 · Invite Management

Segmented invite lists and registration management for each event

Built and segmented invite lists by persona and seniority — dinner lists looked very different from conference contact lists.

  • Guest list segmentation by role & seniority
  • RSVP & registration tracking
  • Format matched to audience objective
02 · Logistics

Venue and travel logistics across London and New York

Coordinated venues and catering for executive dinners in London, and full travel logistics for the leadership team's New York trip.

  • Venue & catering coordination (LDN)
  • Full travel logistics (NYC trip)
  • Cross-border event planning
03 · On-Site Execution

On-the-ground support at conferences and executive dinners

Supported the team on-site at conferences — managing the booth, distributing brochure library, and capturing leads in real time.

  • Booth management at SRP Europe
  • Brochure library distribution
  • Real-time lead capture (no business cards lost)
04 · CRM & Follow-Up

Every contact tagged in Zoho CRM with a follow-up plan attached

Logged every contact in Zoho CRM straight after each event, tagged by event, city, and content pillar, with follow-up tasks assigned to sales.

  • 100% of contacts logged post-event
  • Tagged by event, city & content pillar
  • Sales follow-up tasks assigned same day

Reflection

Format is a strategic decision made before anything else — choosing between conference presence and executive dinner changed the guest list, the venue, and the talking points. And CRM hygiene is the difference between an event that feeds pipeline and one that disappears into someone's inbox.
