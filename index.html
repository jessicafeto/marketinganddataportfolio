Books & Ideas

Some of the books that have shaped how I think about business, people, decision-making and growth.

An idea I keep coming back to
“The shoe that fits one person pinches another; there is no recipe for living that suits all cases.”
Carl Jung, Modern Man in Search of a Soul. I first came across it in The Personal MBA.

It is a useful corrective in this work. The segment that responds to one campaign ignores the next, the metric that matters to one business is noise to another, and the model that performs well on one dataset fails on the next. Most of the job is working out which case you are actually in before deciding what to do about it.
