About the project

A name that means soul, given a world to live in.

AMË began with a conviction: Albanian craft and Albanian places deserve a fashion house of their own. The challenge was to express heritage without nostalgia — to make something that belongs on a Paris shelf and in a Krujë courtyard at the same time.

The identity is anchored by a handwritten signature: personal, imperfect, permanent — the way a maker signs their work. Around it sits a quiet system of stone, cream and ink drawn directly from the places the clothes come from.

Creative direction carried the identity into the world: campaigns photographed in historic hillside towns, garments in motion against fortress walls and cobbled streets, and a social presence that reads like an editorial, not a feed.

What we delivered

Brand identity

The signature wordmark in ink, stone and white; an embroidery version for garments; and the restrained palette and typography that hold the world together.

Creative direction

Campaign concepts built around heritage towns — Krujë, Lezhë, Korçë — with wardrobe, casting and location direction that keeps every frame in the same quiet world.

Campaign photography

An editorial series across generations — couples, families, elders — photographed in soft, overcast light with the landscape as a second protagonist.

Social media

A grid designed as a continuous editorial: campaign frames, garment details and typography posts sequenced with intention.

Results

A fashion brand with a soul — and a system.

AMË launched with a complete world: identity, campaign, wardrobe of assets and a social presence that feels collected rather than posted. The signature now lives on garments as embroidery, and every new drop has a visual language it can step straight into.
