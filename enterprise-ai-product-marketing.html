PRODUCT MARKETING

Positioning enterprise AI products for executive audiences.

One of my core responsibilities was transforming highly technical AI capabilities into marketing materials that enterprise clients could quickly understand. Rather than explaining algorithms or technical architecture, the focus was on communicating business value, customer outcomes and practical use cases.

Every brochure, presentation and solution brief was designed to simplify complex technology while maintaining credibility for senior stakeholders across investment banks and financial institutions.

PRODUCT POSITIONING

Turning technical capabilities into compelling commercial messaging.

Working closely with leadership and product teams, I developed messaging that explained enterprise AI solutions across collateral management, securities processing, reconciliation, prime brokerage and intelligent automation.

This work ensured every marketing asset communicated consistent value propositions, customer benefits and commercial positioning across digital, print and event materials.

SALES ENABLEMENT

Creating commercial assets that supported enterprise conversations.

Beyond product marketing, I produced sales enablement materials that helped the commercial team communicate solutions during meetings, demonstrations and executive events.

These included product brochures, solution briefs, one-pagers, capability decks, executive presentations and supporting visuals used throughout the sales process.

FEATURED DELIVERABLES

Enterprise-ready marketing assets designed for product launches, executive meetings and client engagement.

Throughout the project I created a growing library of commercial marketing assets, including enterprise brochures, solution briefs, product collateral, event materials, presentations and supporting sales documentation used across the customer journey.

SOLUTION BRIEFS

Explaining complex AI solutions through clear, executive-focused documentation.

Solution briefs became one of the most effective ways to communicate enterprise AI capabilities. Each document translated technical functionality into business outcomes, making products easier to understand for executives, buyers and prospective clients.

Every piece balanced clarity with credibility, combining concise messaging, structured layouts and visual storytelling to support enterprise sales conversations.

COMMERCIAL COLLATERAL

Supporting enterprise campaigns across digital and physical touchpoints.

Alongside product marketing, I developed a wide range of supporting commercial assets used throughout campaigns, client meetings and executive events. These materials ensured a consistent visual identity and messaging across every customer interaction.

Deliverables included brochures, banners, event graphics, capability sheets, presentations, invitations and printed collateral.

EXECUTIVE EVENTS

Bringing enterprise AI products into real-world conversations.

Marketing extended beyond digital channels into industry conferences and executive networking events, where product messaging needed to remain consistent across exhibition stands, printed materials and supporting presentations.

These experiences strengthened the connection between product marketing, brand communication and enterprise relationship building.
